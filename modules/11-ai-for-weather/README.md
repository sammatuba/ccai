# Module 11 — AI for Weather

**Tutorial:** PiggyCast — Stacking-Based Ensemble Weather Prediction

Builds a stacking ensemble (XGBoost meta-model) on top of physics-based and AI weather
forecasts (GraphCast, Pangu, NeuralGCM, IFS HRES) to improve medium-range Z500 forecasts.
PiggyCast leads at lead times ≥ 120 h; NeuralGCM is stronger at short leads.
