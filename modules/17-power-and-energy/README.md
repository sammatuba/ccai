# Module 17 — AI for Power & Energy Systems

**Tutorial:** AI for AC Optimal Power Flow

Solving a 5-bus AC-OPF problem, generating solver labels with PYPOWER, training PyTorch
surrogate models, and evaluating with OPF-aware metrics (feasibility, cost gap, runtime,
model size, training emissions). Key lesson: low MSE ≠ a feasible dispatch.

- Notebook: [AI_for_Optimal_Power_Flow.ipynb](AI_for_Optimal_Power_Flow.ipynb)
