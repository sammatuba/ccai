# Module 15 — AI for Oceans & Marine Systems

**Tutorial:** Monitoring Coastal Water Quality and Marine Ecosystem Change

Satellite remote sensing (Copernicus Marine) + ML over the Northern Adriatic: Chl-a, NDCI,
and turbidity indicators; Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, and a patch-based CNN, with
careful attention to independent inputs (avoiding circularity) and temporal-generalization
limitations.

- Notebook: [Monitoring_Coastal_Water_Quality.ipynb](Monitoring_Coastal_Water_Quality.ipynb)
- Source: https://github.com/climatechange-ai-tutorials/coastal-water-quality (MIT License)
